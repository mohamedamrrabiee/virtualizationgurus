One validated configuration in. A full VCF 9.1.1 management domain out. That is what the VCF Installer gives you.

Behind a single Deploy click, the installer runs seven milestones and more than 280 tracked steps: vCenter, SDDC Manager, the cluster with its distributed switch and vSAN datastore, three NSX Managers deployed in parallel, and the three VCF 9 layers on top. Done by hand, that is days of sequenced, error-prone work. Here it is one guided, repeatable workflow.

→ Every dependency is built in the right order, with a step count per stage so you always know where you are
→ A failed step pauses the stage and keeps state, so you fix the cause and retry instead of rebuilding
→ The same validated input gives the same result every time, which is what makes it consistent across environments

The Payoff:
The installer does not remove the need for preparation. It rewards it, because the better the input, the less there is to fix.
